# GEO worklist for Afar Horizon

Prepared 11 October 2026. Separate follow-up to the English SEO phases; no AI-visibility outcome has been measured or promised. Phase 6 launch dependencies remain open.

Google's AI Search guidance retains ordinary SEO foundations: accessible pages, useful textual content, internal links, suitable images and schema consistent with visible information. It does not require an additional AI-specific file or schema. This guidance concerns Google Search; it is not a guarantee about other answer engines. [Google AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).

| Order | Work | Existing starting point | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Launch and verify crawl/index eligibility | Phase 6 checklist | Live checks and Search Console URL inspection |
| 2 | Add concise answers to real traveller questions within current page styles | FAQ, Before You Go, Semera, packing and Erta Ale guides | Operator-reviewed answers with relevant destination/package links |
| 3 | Add first-hand operational expertise where the client can substantiate it | Current Afar articles and package details | Real reviewer, approved specifics, accurate dates and photo subjects |
| 4 | Keep the business identity and citations consistent | About/contact, shared business schema, real profiles once supplied | Confirmed profile URLs; schema matches visible facts |
| 5 | Keep scientific/historical claims traceable and current | Dallol, Erta Ale, Hadar and cultural destination content | Primary references plus dated review of changeable statements |
| 6 | Assess useful visits and enquiries alongside visibility samples | Search Console, Vercel page views, operator enquiry aggregates | Dated observations and real metrics with source limitations stated |

When checking answer engines manually, use a small stable list of questions (for example, planning a private Danakil trip from Semera or choosing between short and longer itineraries). Record engine, date, question, returned citations and whether those citations describe the business accurately. Results can vary across time, location and accounts; a sample answer is not a ranking report or conversion attribution. Google AI-feature traffic is included in overall Web search performance in Search Console; do not invent a separate AI-only series from that aggregate.

Collect operator material before rewriting. Preserve intentional reviews, company facts and prices, and keep the Afar-led editorial balance. No new `llms.txt`, fabricated credentials, special schema, automated answer-engine queries or third-party account changes are part of this preparation.
