## Evidence and remaining confirmations

- Erta Ale wording was checked against [Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=221080). Its dated 2024–2025 reports describe changing volcanic activity; they do not establish conditions for a future departure. Removed the unsupported claim that no lava lake exists at present and the claim that lava-lake activity never returned after 2017.
- Lalibela was checked against [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/18/), which describes continuing pilgrimage use. Fixed visiting-hour, ceremony, light and lodge-service promises were removed.
- [FCDO travel advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/ethiopia/safety-and-security) reinforces that access and regional circumstances need current checks. Route availability, camp locations and walking distances still require date-specific confirmation by the operator; editorial review is not operational approval.
- Reviewed all 29 distinct Cloudinary photo subjects in a contact sheet. The supplied `dallol.png` visibly shows lava; Dallol-labelled editorial uses now reuse the mineral-pool image from `danakil-depression.png`. Intentional review imagery was preserved. Alt text describes visible subjects. The supplied filename is not independent proof of exact location, date or image rights; those remain client provenance confirmations.
- Privacy and website-terms pages were requested during this phase and are based on current forms, assistant storage and service integrations. No invented refund deadlines, data-retention periods or compliance certifications were added. Booking conditions and actual production providers/retention remain operator-owned information.
- Actual social/review account URLs are still needed. Existing placeholder profile links are preserved until supplied; no external accounts or legal credentials were invented.
